How the numbers work

Bookmaker 1X2 odds are converted to probabilities and the bookmaker margin is removed proportionally, so home, draw and away add up to 100%. Where no bookmaker prices a match (for example the Russian, Iranian and UAE leagues), a model estimate is shown instead: a Poisson model built from each team's goals scored and conceded per game this season, with home advantage. Model estimates are marked and are less reliable than odds. "Not to lose" is the favorite's win-or-draw chance. "Win odds" is the bookmaker's decimal price for the favorite. These are estimates, not guarantees: even an 80% favorite fails to win about one match in five. Please bet responsibly, if at all.
