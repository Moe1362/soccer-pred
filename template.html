How the numbers work

Bookmaker 1X2 odds are converted to probabilities and the bookmaker margin is removed proportionally, so home, draw and away add up to 100%. "Not to lose" is the favorite's win-or-draw chance. "Win odds" is the bookmaker's decimal price for the favorite. These are estimates, not guarantees: even an 80% favorite fails to win about one match in five. Please bet responsibly, if at all.
