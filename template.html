Method: bookmaker 1X2 odds (usually DraftKings, via ESPN) are converted to probabilities and the bookmaker margin is removed proportionally so the three outcomes add up to 100%. "Not to lose" is the favorite's win-or-draw chance. "Win odds" is the bookmaker's own decimal price for the favorite. All times are US Pacific (PT). Match status reflects the last update. These numbers are estimates, not guarantees: even an 80% favorite fails to win about one match in five.
